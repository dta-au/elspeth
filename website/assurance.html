Review the evidence

Understand what happened.
Know what the record can establish.

ELSPETH records processing evidence as a workflow runs. Use that record to investigate outputs and failures, alongside the source material, your evaluation criteria and human review.

The Landscape audit trail.

Follow a row through the workflow

Landscape records source and token lineage, route decisions, external calls and terminal outcomes. This lets a reviewer trace how an output relates to its input and processing steps.

Keep operational logs in context

Logs and telemetry help operators diagnose service behaviour. Landscape is the runtime evidence store; operational logs are not a substitute for the audit record.

Checks, evidence and their limits.

What a check tells you
Control or record Purpose Still requires review
Graph and schema validation Detect invalid configuration and incompatible connections. Whether the configured process answers the intended question.
Lineage and call records Trace processing, routing and recorded provider interactions. Source quality, interpretation and the correctness of generated content.
Audit export and optional signing Support evidence export and integrity checking under the configured policy. Key custody, retention, access and completeness of the evidence needed for your use case.

Recoverable publication

Supported sinks record publication intent before external publication. If publication is interrupted, recovery uses the stored effect records and the sink's documented protocol.

This is a bounded recovery mechanism. It does not promise exactly-once effects for every external system. Follow the sink-effect recovery runbook for the supported cases and operator checks.

People remain responsible for the use.

Evaluate before relying on outputs

ELSPETH is an AI-generated and AI-reviewed prototype in active development. Major systems can change, and user testing has covered a limited set of use cases. It is not ready for general production use.

Use representative evaluation data, review errors and establish an escalation process. LLM output can be inaccurate or unsupported even when a pipeline executes successfully.

Plan the operating controls

Decide who may author and execute workflows, where data may be sent, what evidence must be retained and how it will be protected. Test failure and recovery procedures for your deployment.

The software does not provide whole-platform certification or determine compliance for your organisation.
