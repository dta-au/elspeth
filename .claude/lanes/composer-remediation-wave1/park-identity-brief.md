Do not normalize loaded row identities, receipt IDs/snapshots, stored proposal argument dictionaries, or saved graphs to repair inconvenient evidence. Owned corruption must still fail. `pending_proposal_reference_id` continues to receive a canonical mutation-service identity and preserve exact acceptance checks. Existing service APIs may still construct UUID objects from admitted canonical strings; avoiding duplicate normalization does not justify changing those APIs.
- Public successful `blob_id` echoes become canonical for formerly noncanonical requests; metadata already returns canonical `id`. Key/envelope names and content paging/hash semantics stay unchanged. This is an intentional response-value correction; no frontend union/envelope redesign is needed.
- Keep malformed argument and unavailable-resource dispositions as they are unless needed solely to avoid falsely resolving an invalid planner information key. Do not fold the broader error-code catalogue or async recovery-repair redesign into this task.
- Exclude BOS-01 pagination, parked-window paging, inspect-source snapshot/failure ownership, encoding-aware inspection-key redesign, model/secret discovery, and general observation completeness. This slice can address BOS-03 and XC-08 plus their identity consequences; it cannot close R2 or XC-13 generally.
