## Sound existing design seams

- Canonical prepare must retain base, proposed/owned authority, candidate acceptance, and recovery pair/hash proofs before equality. For owned proposals, a normalized candidate that differs from proposed authority remains integrity failure; it is not a harmless no-op. After those proofs, NO_CHANGE can carry a previously validated recovery binding and avoid both fresh execution and recovered-success publication.
- Committed replay is already before preparation in `pipeline_settlement.py:349–391`; repeated rejection is already terminal at `393–394`. Preserve these first. A no-change proposal is pending-to-rejected, never a replacement committed state or a reinterpretation of an existing committed receipt.
- `PipelineDispatchAuditBinding` is explicitly SUCCESS-only (`pipeline_commit.py:145`); `_persist_tool_invocations` only returns bindings for SUCCESS set_pipeline (`routes/_helpers.py:2069`). An executor ToolArgumentError must stay one recorder ARG_ERROR invocation, persisted through the buffer with no `exc.invocation` success-binding contract and no manufactured content hash. Build-time ToolArgumentError has zero executor invocations. Precise wrapping of those two exception sites is adequate; broader ValueError/integrity normalization is not.
- Recovery lookup validates the actual same-call stored successful envelope, arguments/content binding and duplicate-success cardinality (`proposal_authority.py:423`, `service.py::_pipeline_dispatch_recovery_on_connection`). The new equality branch must not bypass the lookup. Failed attempts preceding a success remain separate evidence, not substitute success bindings.
- Existing `PipelineCommitError` cleanup already selects validated recovery binding first and persists buffered invocations before required rejection. Extending its reason/public-projection mapping is the smallest seam. `reject_pipeline_with_required_custody` in `sessions/pipeline_rejection_custody.py:91` checks privately issued actual SQL outcome, performs real projection, retains post-SQL faults/cancellations, and only then returns. New 422 outcomes belong after that completion; direct legacy rejection would lose those guarantees.

- The planner's finalized `candidate_state` is produced with canonical review authority and rewritten inline custody together (`pipeline_planner.py:3064–3165`). It is the correct comparison object. Publisher equality after complete audit persistence and before Stage2/create/intent is valid for explicit, auto and snapshot-auto/live-explicit modes. No new trust read is needed for a no-op; changed plans retain existing snapshot/live trust intersection and green Stage2 rule. Candidate absence remains existing not-run staging.

Real planner terminal tests must still assert successful DONE and unchanged provider/repair counts, rather than treating equality as candidate rejection.
