## Quota: reuse evaluation, never reserve

`RepositoryQuotaAuthority.admit_storage_bytes`677 / `admit_storage_bytes_on_connection`808 already evaluate and lock policies without allocating a resource/reservation. Reuse that evaluator with the real `state._quota_exceeded_recorder`; if naming is clarified, keep existing callers forwarding to exactly the same implementation. Do not duplicate just its arithmetic or replace the recorder with `lambda: None`. Preserve normal attempt and quota-refusal audit. `refuse_unrecorded_quota_exceeded`708 deliberately faults without a recorder.

Replacement requires **both** current checks:

- Session: `session_storage_bytes_on_connection`758 includes all session blobs plus output reservation excess. Require `current_total - old.size_bytes + replacement.size_bytes <= max_storage_per_session`, exactly as prepare/commit do. This check still runs for zero/negative delta and may refuse when the projected session remains over cap.
- Identity/container: pass delta `replacement.size_bytes - old.size_bytes`, operation `blob_replacement`, to the shared authority. Positive growth locks active identity/container policies; usage includes other owned sessions and other identities for container accounting. Revoked formerly-issued identity allowance fails StorageAccountingUnavailableError; never-issued identity may use container-only regime; no storage policies means no invented bound. SQL accounting errors fail unavailable without invented measured usage. IdentityStorageQuotaExceededError records exact cap/ceiling/usage/scope before refusal (identity wins when both exceeded); it is a BlobQuotaExceededError. StorageAccountingUnavailableError is a distinct operational category, not generic quota-exceeded success/refusal text.
- Output reservations: aggregates729-780 add `max(reservation.size_bytes - SUM(blobs.size_bytes at target_path), 0)`, charging unmaterialized output while avoiding full double counting of already covered bytes. Use these functions, not SUM(blobs) or file sizes.
- Nonpositive delta: after exact session-owner existence lookup, identity/container evaluator returns usage/cap/ceiling None without measuring/locking policies, even if identity revoked or already over those bounds. Preserve this; do not generalize the bypass to session cap, authority, retention, canonical bytes or metadata readiness. Delete produces no growth and does not acquire a new quota gate.

Preview creates no replacement/deletion intent, blob row, output/inline reservation, provenance update, applied effect receipt, file, rename/unlink or cleanup. Locks and approved audit evidence are allowed. Acceptance retains both prepare and commit quota evaluation; no reservation or quota promise survives preview.
