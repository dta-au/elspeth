> **CONTRACT DEVIATION 1: `get_for_turn`, an additive request-bearing read.**
> - **Clauses in tension.** Contract § Authority says "poll reads never select `request_json`", and § Owned types says
>   `request_json: str | None  # poll reads load it as None (never selected)`. The contract names one point read,
>   `get`. N13 uses `get` as the poll read and asserts `record.request_json is None` on a queued row
>   (`N13.md:772`). N06 says `get` "leaves `request_json` unloaded" (`N06.md:57-58`). N12's Step 1 probe expects
>   `get` to return the body so the worker can rebuild the turn, and it says so itself: "`get_returns_request_json
>   False`: N05's `get` is the poll read. Add a request-bearing read in N05 (and a `CONTRACT DEVIATION:` there)"
>   (`N12.md:299-300`).
> - **Smallest change.** `get` stays the poll read. A new
>   `get_for_turn(*, session_id: UUID, operation_id: str) -> ComposerOperationRecord | None` selects every column and
>   loads `request_json`. It is a read, so it adds one `_REVIEWED_READ_CONNECTIONS` row and no writer row.
> - **Ripple.** N12's Step 1 probe and its worker call `get_for_turn` where they need the body. Every other
>   consumer is unchanged.

> **CONTRACT DEVIATION 2: the owner's `request_cancelled` terminal requires the marker.**
> - **Clauses in tension.** Contract § Authority keys `settle_composer_operation_on_connection` on
>   `cancel_requested_at IS NULL`. Contract § Composite terminal has `fail_composer_async_operation(...)` with
>   `audit_only=(failure.failure_code == "request_cancelled")`. Spec § 4 says: "If cancel commits first, completion is
>   rejected … and the worker settles `request_cancelled` after persisting the LLM-call audit cohort". Under a strict
>   `IS NULL`, the owner can never publish a committed Stop. The row would stay `running` until the reaper turned it
>   into `worker_lost` or `request_cancelled` a lease later.
> - **Smallest change.** An outcome whose `failure_code == "request_cancelled"` requires `cancel_requested_at IS NOT
>   NULL`. Every other outcome (the success DTO and every other failure) requires `IS NULL`. A refused CAS is re-read
>   and classified in this order:
>   1. not the exact running row → `ComposerOperationFenceLost`;
>   2. a `request_cancelled` outcome with no marker → `ValueError` (a caller defect);
>   3. any other outcome with a marker → `ComposerOperationCancelledDuringTurn`.
> - **Ripple.** None. N07's `fail_composer_async_operation` already passes the `request_cancelled` envelope only
>   after a committed marker.

> **CONTRACT DEVIATION 3: `admit_composer_operation` takes explicit keywords, not `**admit_kwargs: object`.**
> - **Clause.** Contract § Authority: `async def admit_composer_operation(authority, *, rate_limit, **admit_kwargs:
>   object)`.
> - **Measured reason.** Forwarding `dict[str, object]` into `admit`'s typed keywords fails mypy, and AGENTS.md
>   forbids `# type: ignore`. It was measured with two 20-line scratch modules, identical except for the forwarding
>   shape, under the repository's mypy 1.20.2 (`.venv/bin/mypy --strict`):
>   - the contract shape printed `Argument 1 to "admit" of "Authority" has incompatible type "**dict[str, object]";
>     expected "UUID"  [arg-type]`, plus four more `arg-type` errors, and `contract_shape exit=1`;
>   - the explicit shape printed `Success: no issues found in 1 source file` and `explicit_shape exit=0`.
>   - Without `--strict` the contract shape still fails (`arg-type` is on by default): `default-config exit=1`.
> - **Smallest change.** The coroutine takes `admit`'s keywords explicitly (T04's shape, plus `base_state_id`):
>   `async def admit_composer_operation(authority, *, rate_limit, session_id, operation_id, kind, request_hash,
