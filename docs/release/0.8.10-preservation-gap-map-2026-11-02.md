- **Outside-primary, unnamed and ignored backup contents remain unverified** beyond the named supplements. The registered-worktree audit did not complete the broad ignored-content traversal. Registry/HEAD accounting is not an inventory of every spare source copy, lane or archive. Dated worktree counts describe their own cuts, not a present deletion or completeness conclusion.

Confidence is high in the named metadata mapping, and the post-cut receipt records complete local readback of its 499 selected members. This documentation update did not independently rerun that readback or reverify older archive bytes.
