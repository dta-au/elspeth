## Remaining implementation findings

### R1 — Nonclean terminal results do not all reach publication

**U40 / shared post-P4 semantic completion. High confidence.**

The real classifier produces `owned_recovery` and `owned_refusal` in [src/elspeth/web/composer/service.py:1397](../../src/elspeth/web/composer/service.py#L1397) and 1613. Following the paid semantic Stop decision, [src/elspeth/web/composer/continuation_semantic_advisor_phase.py:195](../../src/elspeth/web/composer/continuation_semantic_advisor_phase.py#L195) routes a ToolBatch terminal through the iteration Stop consumer. That consumer invokes `_clean_iteration_return_for_stop` at [src/elspeth/web/sessions/continuation_semantic_advisor.py:2234](../../src/elspeth/web/sessions/continuation_semantic_advisor.py#L2234). Its guard at 2057–2068 requires `model_end`, clean END disposition and the complete authenticated END/publication binding.

**Trigger and consequence:** an applied P4 batch followed by an original owned recovery/refusal and semantic `STOP_CONFIRMED` reaches an integrity rejection instead of publishing its intended terminal response. The atomic P4 writer exists; this finding concerns the subsequent completion branch.

**Working-tree progress:** the new terminal consumer, owned-publication admission and graph-recovery projection form a substantive repair, with a real paid-recovery/Stop test. However, the working-tree `read_original_owned_iteration_return_on_connection` at `src/elspeth/web/sessions/continuation_iteration_return.py:1780` explicitly accepts only `owned_recovery` with graph-recovery intent. Its reviewed SHA-256 was `2a99581a0d1b77e9f4a3bd4bd594353dd5d77368c3467b865525295663d45777`. This does not close `owned_refusal`.

**Next work:** complete kind-specific refusal publication with original source/exception ownership; review and integrate the recovery repair; qualify both through the actual semantic completion path. Do not rebuild the already implemented P4 transaction.

**U32, with U31/U40 caller integration. High confidence.**

The prescribed semantic Read path supplies the configured policy. The ordinary PRIMARY tool path does not:

Hash verification still runs at line 2025; this is not a claim that all blob integrity checks disappeared.

**Next work:** join the current inspection policy and retained observations to ordinary PRIMARY attachment execution, preferably through the intended retained attachment owner, and test the same exact-limit/refusal behavior through both real caller families.
