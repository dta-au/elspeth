# 0.8.10 paid-result envelope contract review

**2 November 2026 — policy decided, implementation and acceptance open.** The installed LiteLLM is `1.102.0` (`uv.lock` and the installed distribution agree). This review inspected the current Composer call and retention owners and the providers' published API contracts. It made no provider call and does not identify the operator's active deployment route. PRIMARY and Advisor model names are configurable; their checked-in defaults are examples of possible routes.

The original 0.8.10 policy required every mandatory paid-result representation to fit a proved bound **before dispatch**. The operator has now approved a narrow exception for usable Continuous Composer, described below. The private-document and evidence limits each remain **1,048,576 bytes**. The aggregate allowance and a temporary memo RAM budget are different limits.

The local `ModelResponse` type admits content, tool calls, reasoning, provider-specific fields and response headers (`.venv/lib/python3.13/site-packages/litellm/types/utils.py`). ELSPETH's response codec retains complete content, tool IDs/names/arguments, reasoning, usage and physical audit (`src/elspeth/web/composer/continuation_codec.py`). TITLE retains its full original response and audit even when its accepted display is short (`src/elspeth/web/composer/continuation_title_provider.py`). The inspected current V2 PRIMARY/classifier, TITLE, manual/checkpoint Advisor and semantic Advisor call sites **do propagate explicit generation-token limits** through LiteLLM; legacy no-budget freeform PRIMARY does not set one. Costed calls can record `finish_reason="length"` as `RESPONSE_TRUNCATED` after earlier cost checks, distinct from storage overflow. These are real controls, but a token maximum, post-return rejection, streaming cancellation, compression or a short parsed display is insufficient evidence for pre-dispatch retention of the complete original result.

## Approved 0.8.10 exception

The operator confirmed that usable Continuous Composer is the patch's required feature and explicitly authorized **terminal paid overflow for provider calls**. A paid provider call may dispatch without a proved complete-result byte bound. Retain the complete result whenever it fits the existing mandatory representations. If it cannot be retained, reserve and write an honest terminal **paid/incomplete-evidence** outcome, retain the known cost or mark it unknown, and never apply missing graph/tool output or automatically repeat that paid call. The outcome must be recoverable as the same physical attempt. This is an acknowledged reduction in content/replay evidence for that exceptional result, not a claim of complete audit. The permission does not raise either 1 MiB limit, alter non-provider tool policy, or make V3 accepted before tests and recovery checks pass.

Four independent read-only reviewers applied solution-architecture, systems-thinking, LLM-contract and integrity lenses, then read one another's reports. They agreed that no inspected route qualifies under the original predispatch guarantee. A durable full-result spill could retain more exceptional evidence but requires a new authoritative storage, accounting, crash-recovery and cold-reader contract; finite storage still cannot promise capture of an unbounded producer. A bounded receipt without the missing bytes cannot support replay or establish provider origin. The approved terminal overflow is the smallest policy change supporting ordinary paid V3 progress; implementation and release qualification remain open. Their working reports are under `.claude/lanes/codex-takeover-20261101/provider-cap-*-panel.md`.
