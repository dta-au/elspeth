# 0.8.17 deferred scope

This is the durable list of work pushed out of 0.8.16 into 0.8.17, plus the work already queued
for 0.8.17. It exists so that nothing deferred during the 0.8.16 release is lost. Update it as
items land, change status or move release; do not delete an entry without recording why.

Started 2026-11-22 on the 0.8.17 integration branch, cut from the 0.8.16 candidate `3e22cc124`.
The branch moves forward to the green 0.8.16 tip by fast-forward or merge, never by a rebase of
pushed commits. No 0.8.17 code is merged yet: this document is the "get things ready" step.

## How to read an entry

Each entry gives what the item is, why it was deferred, its status, an evidence pointer and, where
one exists, a revisit trigger.

- **Status.** *Ruled*: the maintainer decided it belongs in 0.8.17. *Integrator's call*: placed in
  0.8.17 by the release integrator without a maintainer ruling. *Pending*: a question is open with
  the maintainer, and the item may still land in 0.8.16. When a pending item lands in 0.8.16,
  update its entry here.
- **Provenance.** *Measured* (from a run), *read* (from the code at the cited lines), *relayed*
  (reported during the release and not re-checked when this list was written), *inferred*.
- **Line numbers** refer to the 0.8.16 candidate unless stated, and drift. Re-locate by symbol
  before building.
- **Evidence** marked "working notes" is held in the integrators' untracked notes under the same
  entry id. Anything a builder needs from those notes is copied into the handoff for that item.
- Every item here still needs the normal release process before it lands: a red test on the exported
  base, every static step of `.github/workflows/ci.yaml`, an independent review, and the whole-tree
  gates in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.

## B. Context budget and shedding

- **B1. Estimate overshoot.** Ruled 2026-11-21 ("Let's slip I for now but make sure it's not lost
  ..."; "However if it becomes a potential issue later we revisit"). The byte arm's rate can only
  get denser (`web/composer/context_budget.py:272-274`); the anchored arm charges one token per
  non-shared byte (`:280-286`); history is never shared (`:254`), so the byte arm wins on 181 of
  182 records. Measured on a live 62-edit session: the first shed estimated 172,875 tokens against
  the provider's 115,258 (1.48x), so history is dropped at a real ~58% of the cap and falls to
  ~43%. Fix candidates: let a plausible anchor move the rate both ways inside the existing
  plausibility band; or price only the non-shared delta at the anchor's density. Risk: content
  denser than the anchor is under-counted; it changes what `refuse` callers refuse, so the
  handoff must list every one. A naive two-way rate under-counts after shedding. Unlanded tests
  from an archived working tree assert that a measured ratio may not lower the admission bound,
  the opposite of the first candidate; reconcile them. Take early, after the 0.8.16
  shed-history-only and retry commits are on main. **Revisit trigger:** an edit that fails, or a
  guess presented as fact, on a turn where the provider's real count was well under the cap.
- **B2. Default request byte ceiling against larger model windows.** Ruled 2026-11-21 ("1 yes, 2
  delay"). `composer_planner_max_request_bytes` defaults to 960 KiB (`web/config.py:397`; confirm
  which setting the compose loop uses). Computed at 4.33 bytes/token, it refuses requests a model
  would take once the window is above ~227k tokens; it does not bite on a 200k-token model. It
  conflicts in principle with "if it fits, it goes". Fix: derive the ceiling from the declared
  window, or raise the default; keep the operator override, and keep any value an operator set
  explicitly. Size defaults are judged against the four direct provider routes. Take with B1.
- **B3. Exhaustive review of the context-budget code, then a review of that review.** Ruled
  2026-11-21 ("When we have capacity ... a more exhaustive review of that code - then we can have
  [a second model] review the review"). Scope, as the integrator reads "that code" (confirm before
  starting; the other reading is the lease code): `web/composer/context_budget.py`,
  `context_budget_apply.py`, the compose-loop and planner shedding call sites (`service.py`,
  `pipeline_planner.py`), advisor age shaping and pricing (`advisor_checkpoint.py`,
  `advisor_transcript.py`), and the refusal and retry paths. Read-only and exhaustive: all three
  seats; estimate against provider counts; marks, order and hysteresis; every accounting guard;
  both growth patterns (work redone over the whole history each turn; pairwise work inside one
  call); what each `on_over_estimate` mode does; the claim that no accounting guard can fire on
  legitimate inputs. Inputs: the compaction design and overshoot notes and the 182 recorded budget
  decisions (working notes). The second review confirms or rejects every finding against the code
  and ranks the survivors; findings are claims until confirmed. Run after the 0.8.16 shedding and
  retry commits are on main, and before building B1 and B2.
- **B4. Retry once with all history shed after a provider context-length rejection.** Exists only
  in the compose loop (4b1029c36). Missing on the planner (`pipeline_planner.py:4393-4405`) and the
  last-chance composition call (`service.py:2810`, where the rejection is not classified as a size
  refusal): integrator's call, 0.8.17. Reply stage (`service.py:2613-2624`) and advisor
  (`advisor_checkpoint.py:721-728`, `1636-1650`): pending, may land in 0.8.16; if unanswered at
  ship, 0.8.17.
- **B5. Unused stub fields.** `stubbed_tool_call_ids` and the stub helpers are always empty or
  unused since 631ace313. Removing them changes a stored shape; proposal only. Stale `tool:` keys
  in `previously_shed` are tolerated.
- **B6. Retry record key.** The retry record carries an optional
  `retry_after_provider_rejection: true` that nothing reads yet. Surface it wherever call audits
  are shown.
- **B7. Compaction battery.** Separate from the counting run: cap 320,000, marks 0.85/0.60, a

  per shedding edit (working notes). Pending: whether it runs before 0.8.16 ships. Its claim that
  no accounting guard can fire (200k fuzz trials) is unchecked; B3 verifies it.
- **B8. Pricing by producer class.** Partly landed: 0.8.16 prices at a flat 3.0 bytes/token or at
  a measured density (709e171ca); per-producer-class pricing did not ship. Take with B1, whose
  estimate it changes.
- **B9. Request capture bound against the request byte ceiling.** Align the bound on the retained
