# 03 — Shared responsibility matrix

**Status:** product baseline complete; Deployment record required · **Reviewed against:** `49c1845085d36811b120ef1c540048463e32aabc` (2026-09-30) · **Owner:** ELSPETH maintainer

This matrix separates controls supplied by ELSPETH from decisions and operations owned by a deploying organisation, duties placed on users, and controls owned by an external service provider. It applies to the product at the reviewed commit. A controlled copy must record the actual deployment in the [Deployment record](#deployment-record--additional-shared-controls).

The allocation reflects ELSPETH's documented limits in [guarantees.md § 7](../release/guarantees.md#7-what-elspeth-does-not-guarantee), [Platform Architecture — Operational boundaries](../release/platform-architecture.md#operational-boundaries), and [SECURITY.md — Scope](../../SECURITY.md#scope).

## 1. Roles and notation

- **ELSPETH product** means controls implemented in the reviewed source and obligations accepted by the ELSPETH maintainer when maintaining that source.
- **Deploying team** means the system owner, platform operators, security administrators and other people who configure and operate an installation.
- **Users** means people who author, review, approve or run workflows through an interface made available by the deploying team.
- **Third-party provider** means an identity, model, database, storage, telemetry, email, hosting or other external service selected by the deploying team.

Key: **P** = provides the control; **C** = configures or selects it; **S** = supports it through product features or documentation; **O** = obliged to act; **—** = no assigned role.
