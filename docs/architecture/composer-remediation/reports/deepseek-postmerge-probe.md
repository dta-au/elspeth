## New / worth a look
1. **auto_commit + unaccepted first proposal piles up proposals** (`s1-noaccept`: 5 pending `set_pipeline`, one turn answering "the pipeline currently saved is empty"). The approval-containment refusal is explicit_approve-only (s2b/s2c refused). Whether auto_commit should refuse a second proposal while the first (invented-source) proposal is pending is a product call; the stale-base problem of the old defect 1 would apply if the user accepts out of order. Not tested whether accepting a later one 409s.
2. **diff_pipeline hint for pending proposals not exercised**, and the request-start semantics make the "what did your last edit change" question unanswerable one turn later. The model explains that well; the tool still cannot serve the question.
3. **Two-in-a-row 409s in the opt-out session** (s4 turns 2 and 3), each costing 45-170 s and the reply; unchanged from the earlier probe, flagged as known.
4. s2c turn 2 produced an `upsert_node` proposal (a narrow single call) rather than `set_pipeline`; the containment accepts both shapes; just record that "one pending proposal" is not always `set_pipeline`.
